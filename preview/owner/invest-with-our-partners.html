Invest in mutual funds with our partners

Apps and platforms that help you buy open-end mutual fund units in Bangladesh after you have compared returns here.

Bini

Pilot partner

Bini is a Bangladesh mutual fund investing app. After you pick a fund on ReturnKoto?, you can use Bini to compare options in one place, complete the investing steps in the app, and start a monthly SIP from about BDT 1,000 or invest a lump sum. Bini also aims to keep documents, portfolio tracking and follow-up in one mobile flow rather than juggling separate AMC channels.

  • Monthly SIP from about BDT 1,000, or one-time lump sum
  • Fund comparison and digital investing steps in the app
  • Website and native apps for Android and iOS

ReturnKoto? does not operate Bini, open your account, or hold your money. Confirm fees, eligible funds and account requirements on Bini before you invest.

Partner FAQ

Can I invest through ReturnKoto?

No. ReturnKoto? does not open accounts or take your money. It shows historical total returns so you can compare funds first. When you are ready to invest, use a partner on this page (or go directly to an asset manager). Rankings stay independent of partnerships.

Who is Bini on ReturnKoto?

Bini is our pilot mutual fund investing partner. It is a Bangladesh app for comparing funds and completing the buy flow, including SIP from about BDT 1,000. Details and account rules live on bini.com.bd and in the Bini apps. See the Bini section above.

Does ReturnKoto? earn money from partners?

ReturnKoto? may earn a distribution share when you invest through a partner. Rankings and returns are independent of partnerships. Partners do not buy higher calculator ranks or star ratings.

Is listing a partner investment advice?

No. A partner listing is not a recommendation of that partner or of any fund. Past results do not predict the future. For research only.
